Pricing and Packages

Let's find what fits your needs! Sometimes we just need quick, updated family photos; and sometimes we have an occasion to celebrate, such as a milestone or an engagement! If the packages below do not address what you are looking for, please reach out and we can collaborate!

Jack Be Little - $150

Sometimes we just need a mini-session! I always found it hard to “watch and wait” for these to be offered. For this reason, you can see calendar availability and book a Jack-Be-Little Mini-Session at your will! These are available on Tuesdays and Fridays. These will not be themed, but maybe a couple of props. This is great for Holiday Photos, Birthday Photos, School Pictures.

  • Session Time: Up to 20 minutes
  • 8 Edited Images
  • One Location
  • No Outfit Changes
  • (Up to 6 people)

Sugar Pie - $225

Sugar Pie! This is my favorite package. It’s just long enough to get those extra poses, and includes features such as the online gallery, print options, and more client input in general. This session really gives us the time to try multiple poses, or something different. It’s definitely more of an experience.

  • Session Time: Up to 45 minutes
  • 15-20 Edited Images
  • One Location
  • Two Outfit Changes
  • Digital Gallery
  • (Up to 6 people)
  • Print Ordering (Add On)

Cinderella - $300

There are moments and milestones in our lives that deserve all the time and attention one has to offer. The Cinderella Package offers attention to ALL the details: careful orchestrating of time, location, seasonal flowers, and fine ensemble details! This is more than a session, it’s an investment project. I would definitely recommend this package for things like Engagement and Senior Sessions.

  • Session Time: Up to 1.5 hours
  • 25-30 Edited Images
  • Two Locations
  • Two+ Outfit Changes
  • Digital Gallery
  • Flash Drive
  • (Up to 10 people)
  • Print Ordering (Add On)
