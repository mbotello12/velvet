Pricing & Packages

Let's find what fits your needs! Sometimes we just need quick, updated family photos; and sometimes we want an occasion to celebrate, such as a milestone or and engagement! If the packages below do not address what you are looking for, please reach out and we can callaborate!

Jack Be Little - $125

Sometimes we just need a mini-session! I always found it hard to “watch and wait” for these to be offered. What if I don’t see it? What if we miss out? When are the next ones? Will I have enough time to shop for outfits? For this reason, you can see calendar availability and book a Jack-Be-Little Mini-Session at your will! These are available one Saturday a month. These will not be themed, but maybe a couple of props. This is great for Holiday Photos, Birthday Photos, School Pictures.

  • 20 minute Session
  • 8 Edited Images
  • One Location
  • No Outfit Changes
  • (Up to 6 people)

Sugar Pie - $200

Sugar Pie! This is my favorite package. It’s just long enough to get those extra poses, and includes features such as the online gallery, print options, and more client input in general. This session really gives us the time to try things that might be cute, or different. It’s definitely more of an experience.

  • 45 minute Session
  • 15-20 Edited Images
  • One Location
  • Two Outfit Changes
  • Digital Gallery
  • (Up to 6 people)
  • Print Ordering (Add On)

Cinderella - $275

There are moments and milestones in our lives that deserve all the time and attention one has to offer. The Cinderella Package offers attention to ALL the details: careful orchestrating of time, location, seasonal flowers, and the phase of the Moon! This is more than a session, it’s an investment project. I would definitely recommend this package for things like Engagement and Senior Sessions.

  • 60 minute Session
  • 25-30 Edited Images
  • Two Location
  • Two+ Outfit Changes
  • Digital Gallery
  • Flash Drive
  • Custom TikTok/Instagram Reel
  • (Up to 10 people)
  • Print Ordering (Add On)
